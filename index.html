A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What to know before you get started.

What do I need to get started?

For a menu audit, use a clear JPG, PNG or WebP photo of your menu. To check profitability, enter your products, selling prices and ingredient costs. Add sales quantities to explore the product matrix.

Can a menu photo tell me which dishes are profitable?

A photo can reveal menu content and presentation, but it cannot reveal your ingredient costs or actual sales. You need to add those numbers for meaningful profitability analysis.

Can I edit the AI suggestions?

Yes. Review and edit suggested descriptions before using them. Check that every ingredient and preparation detail is accurate for the dish you serve.

Can I create a menu to print?

The current app includes a design and print workflow. You can review your wording and presentation before export. Check the current in-app access requirements and proofread the final menu before printing.

Do you guarantee more sales or profit?

No. The app helps you make more informed menu decisions. Your results depend on your costs, pricing, customers and how you apply the recommendations. Ingredient margin is not the same as net profit.

Are the monthly plans available yet?

Not yet. Basic, Pro and Signature are proposed packages. Prices, usage limits, subscription terms and final features will be confirmed before paid subscriptions open. The current app may show an earlier access model.
