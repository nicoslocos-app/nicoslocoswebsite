A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

The practical details, before you get started.

What do I need to get started?

For a menu scan, upload a clear JPG, PNG or WebP image (for a PDF, export the page as an image), or use the camera option on your phone or tablet. To calculate margins, add selling prices and ingredient costs. You can start with a single product.

What does the Menu Scanner actually tell me?

It reviews the menu’s presentation, structure, wording and descriptions. You get a quality score, category scores and suggestions for improvement. It cannot work out your actual ingredient costs or sales from a photo. AI suggestions should always be checked.

How are product margins calculated?

Selling price minus ingredient cost gives the contribution per sale. Divide that contribution by the selling price to get the ingredient margin percentage. Enter prices excluding VAT. This is not net profit: labour, rent and other operating costs are not deducted. Cocktail costs also include a fixed €0.10 allowance for ice and garnish.

What happens when an ingredient price changes?

Update the purchase price in Ingredients. Products linked to that ingredient through their recipes use the updated cost, so their margins reflect the change. Recipe quantities and units matter — check that they match what you actually serve.

What are Stars, Plowhorses, Puzzles and Dogs?

The product matrix compares contribution per sale and sales popularity within a comparison group. Enter units sold over the last 30 days for at least two products in the same group. The labels help you explore what to promote, review or reprice; they are relative to your products, not an industry benchmark.

Can I create and export a menu?

Yes. Start from scratch or use saved products, edit descriptions, choose a template and add your logo, colours and footer. Export a menu for print or screen. Proofread the text, prices and dietary information before using it.

Where is my beta data saved?

The current beta saves products, ingredients and menus in your browser on the device you use. There are no personal accounts or cross-device sync yet. Clearing browser data or switching browsers can make saved information unavailable. Keep a separate copy of important business information.

What if a menu scan fails?

The AI service can occasionally be busy or unavailable. Wait a little and try again. Use a clear, readable menu file. A failed scan does not mean there is anything wrong with your menu.

Do you guarantee more sales or profit?

No. Nicoslocos helps you make informed decisions about your menu, costs and pricing. Results depend on your restaurant, customers and how you apply the suggestions.
