CLIENT PROJECT / BRAND IDENTITY

A harbour identity.
With character.

A nautical visual identity for the bar at Helsinki’s Katajanokka terminal — built around its setting, with a weathered character that feels right at home by the sea.

About the project +
THE IDEA

Rooted in its surroundings.

The identity brings together an anchor-led logo, condensed lettering and a maritime colour palette. The distressed details give the visual language a lived-in feel, while the blue, white and red keep it distinctive and easy to recognise.

A repeat pattern combines ships, the terminal, waves, cocktails and the Bar K8 logo. The system carries across menus, takeaway cups, coasters, staff clothing and social templates.
